# Obertà — Steam store page (English)

## Game name

Obertà

## Short description (max 300 characters, currently 276)

A noir social deduction game played online. Twelve players, one city: the Family kills at night, the Town argues and votes by day, and at dawn the paper tells all. Ranked tables by language, private rooms with friends, 21 roles. Free to play: coins are only earned by playing.

## About this game (Steam BBCode)

```
[h2]One city. One night. No innocents.[/h2]
Obertà is a noir social deduction game. Around a green felt table sit twelve players: the Family knows each other and strikes at night, the Town has to work out who is lying before it is too late. Every crime makes the front page.

[h2]How a round works[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Night.[/b] The Family picks its victim in a private chat no one else can read. The Detective investigates, the Doctor protects, the others make their moves in secret.
[*][b]Dawn.[/b] The newspaper hits the street: who died, how, and what the town saw.
[*][b]Discussion.[/b] The square opens. Accuse, defend yourself, bluff in the town chat, with numbered seats so everyone knows who is talking.
[*][b]Vote.[/b] Point at a suspect: the hands on the table show who votes for whom. The majority decides who takes the fall, and their card is turned over.
[/list]
Then night falls again, until the Town has cleared the streets or the Family runs the city.

[h2]Ranked: twelve strangers, one table[/h2]
Ranked games are always twelve players. Pick your table by language, Italian or English, and wait in a room where nobody has a name yet: you only find out who you are sitting with when the game starts. Every game moves your rank, from Picciotto to Don. Quitting or going idle earns nothing, not even when your side wins.

[h2]Rooms with friends[/h2]
Open a room and share the code: friends join from their own computer or phone and take a seat. Chat in the lobby, fill empty seats with bots if you are short, start when everyone is in. From 4 to 12 players.

[h2]Twenty-one roles, three factions[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The Family:[/b] Soldier, Godfather, Tailor, Accomplice.
[*][b]The Town:[/b] Detective, Doctor, Sniper, Reporter, Gravedigger, Bodyguard, Veteran, Ex-Con, Messiah, Mayor, Doorman and the Honest Citizens.
[*][b]On their own:[/b] Fool, Arsonist, Bounty Hunter, Loan Shark, Hitman. Each one plays for a victory of their own.
[/list]
Every role is explained in a few plain sentences, and the roles in play are always on screen.

[h2]The tailor and the barber[/h2]
Get your face done at the barber: cuts, moustaches, scars. The tailor's shop has hats, suits, accessories, frames and full looks. Coins are only earned by playing and levelling up: there is nothing to buy with real money, and nothing you wear changes the game. Free to play, no pay-to-win.

[h2]Cross-play with iPhone[/h2]
Same rooms, same ranked queue, same account on PC, Mac and iPhone. You play on one device at a time: sign in on another and the first one steps aside.

[h2]Built for the desktop[/h2]
The table fills the screen: players around the felt, the roles in play and the town chat always open on the right. Full keyboard support, fullscreen or borderless window.
```

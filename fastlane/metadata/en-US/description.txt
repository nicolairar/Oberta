One city. One night. No innocents.

Obertà is a noir social deduction and bluffing game for groups of friends: the Family kills at night, the Town investigates by day, and at dawn the paper tells it all.

ONE PHONE FOR EVERYONE
No cards, no moderator: the phone goes hand to hand while a narrator voices the night, tells the crimes and walks you through the vote. Works offline and without an account.

ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
Open a room, share the code or QR and everyone plays on their own phone. A fixed table, timed turns, a private Family chat at night and an open square to argue in by day.

RANKED (COMING SOON)
Matches with strangers, ratings and a leaderboard to climb: coming soon.

TWENTY-ONE ROLES
Godfather, Tailor, Detective, Doctor, Sniper, Journalist, Undertaker, Bodyguard, Messiah, Mayor, Arsonist, Hitman, Loan Shark and more. Each role is explained in a few plain sentences.

THE BARBER AND THE SHOP
Get your face done at the barber: cuts, moustaches, scars. The shop has hats, suits, accessories, frames and full looks. Coins are only earned by playing: there is nothing to buy with real money.

Real cities, different eras, a new story every game. Tonight, someone won't make it home.

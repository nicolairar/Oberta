SIGN-IN
No demo account is needed.
- One-phone mode ("Un telefono per tutti" / "One phone for everyone", the poster in the middle of the home screen) works fully offline, without any account.
- Online features (rooms with friends, shop, barber) need an account: tap "ACCEDI" / "SIGN IN" on the home screen, then "Sign in with Apple" with your own Apple ID, or "Sign in with email". Email sign-in has no password: type any address you can read, we email a 6-digit code (valid 15 minutes) and a link; type the code in the six boxes (iOS also offers it above the keyboard), or tap "Open Obertà" in the email on the same iPhone and the app opens signed in. On first sign-in you choose a unique username; the barber's mirror then lets you pick a face (it can be skipped).
- To play an online game, open "Gioca con amici" / "Play with friends", tap "OPEN A ROOM" and let a second device signed in with another account (another Apple ID, or email sign-in with another address) join with "JOIN WITH A CODE" (or the QR). A game needs at least 4 players to start; with two devices the room's lobby already shows the table, the chat and the report and block actions below.

COINS AND PURCHASES
The app has no in-app purchases and no real-money currency. Coins are earned only by playing online matches and are spent in the in-game shop on cosmetic items (hats, outfits, frames).

USER-GENERATED CONTENT, REPORT AND BLOCK
Online rooms have a text chat (open square by day, private Family chat at night) and optional push-to-talk voice, only with the people in the same room. Voice is live only and never recorded. Every chat message is checked server-side against a blocked-words filter before it is stored; blocked messages are rejected. Usernames go through the same filter.
To report or block another player (two accounts in the same room, see SIGN-IN):
1. At the table: tap the other player's face, in the room's lobby or during a game (during the day you can also long-press it and choose "Report or block").
2. In the chat: long-press any message written by another player.
3. From a profile: "Play with friends" > "FRIENDS", tap a friend, a friend request or a search result to open their public file; "BLOCK" and "REPORT" are at the bottom.
Paths 1 and 2 open the player's sheet with "Block" and "Report". "Report" asks for a reason (offensive language, cheating or abandoning games, inappropriate username or look, spam, other) and an optional note, then "Send"; a reported chat message is attached to the report. The reported player never learns who reported them. "Block" asks for confirmation: their messages disappear, the friendship ends, friend requests are refused both ways and they can no longer join rooms you host.
Blocked players are listed, and can be unblocked, in Settings (gear icon on the home screen) > "PRIVACY & DATA" > "Blocked players". The community rules and the contact address for urgent reports (oberta@charliemobile.dev) are in Settings > "PRIVACY & DATA" > "Community rules". We review every report.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings (gear icon on the home screen) > "Elimina account" / "Delete account". After typing the confirmation word, the account, profile, friends, wardrobe and coins are deleted permanently and the Sign in with Apple token is revoked.
